## New
- A random table row that links its own table rolls that table again without itself, for rows such as "Mithral + reroll"
- Generators: a note table whose rows are parts rolls them into one result, with a reroll and a lock per part, Give for results with a Type and Save as note; a link to a generator rolls all of it
- Scenes with several floors: add, name and stack them in the command palette's Scene levels page, and switch floors at the map's left edge or with Page Up and Page Down
- Each floor has its own map, tokens, walls, lights, fog and explored areas, and a floor's map is fitted to the scene's grid when you align it there
- Show players a floor with the eye in the floor switcher, and move tokens to another floor from their right-click menu
- Stairs between floors: a token put down on them goes to the floor they lead to, and an elevator lets you pick the floor
- Openings: draw a hole in a floor from the lighting menu to see the floor below through it; a token put down over it falls through
- Transparent parts of a floor's map show the floors below, two deep
- Roofs: give a floor a roof image, and it fades while a player character stands under it
- Import a building's Universal VTT files together as one scene with a floor each
- A pin that links a scene of several floors can open it on the floor you pick
- Animated maps: WebM and MP4 videos play as maps, on the map and in the player window; Animate maps in Atlas' settings shows their first frame instead
- Replace map image: put a video, a gridless version or a larger export of the same map in its place, keeping the grid, walls, lights and tokens

## Improved

- The update notice comes back each time Obsidian starts until you click it, instead of showing only once

## Important changes

- A scene with several floors is saved in a newer format that older versions of Atlas cannot open; scenes with one floor are saved as before
