## New

- Several selected tokens can be turned together with the button above their selection

## Improved

- Resource wheels stay beside their token when it is selected, instead of rising above it
- Selected tokens are outlined by their own shape, crisp at any zoom, with a light frame around a group
- Rulers, templates and drag rulers are drawn as one clean line in the selection's style, with a sharp distance label at every zoom
- The token whose turn it is stands in a soft pool of light with four fine arcs, sharp at any zoom, and no longer shows two rings when selected

## Fixed

- Maps work in a scene moved to its own window
- Dice in statblock tables can be rolled
- A statblock's armour class written with a note, like 15 (natural armor), now shows on its token
- Holding Backspace in a statblock's ability no longer erases the ability above it
- Random tables from notes can be added when a collection already has tables
- Statblock previews on the map can be moved and pinned again, and their images shown to players
- Pinned previews can no longer be dragged out of reach, and statblock previews also move by their name
- @ references in statblocks also find a value by its label, such as @str for Strength

## Atlas Online

- A campaign is played only while its online table is open: choose it when you start the table
- A player's character sheet online is laid out as the GM's statblock card: the same rows, columns and boxes at the GM's card width, scaled to fit, with every ability list on its own heading or tab in the Abilities tab
- Players can add words and labels with values (such as Experiences) to their character's lists online, and take them out again
- Dice a player writes online with @ references, such as 2d8 + @str, roll at once with the numbers filled in
